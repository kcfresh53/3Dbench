# Roadscape attribution

Original library: Roadscape 4.13.10, supplied locally as `Roadscape 4 (Release).blend` and its texture library.
Original authors: Baptiste Mollicone and SLX (https://github.com/slluxx), as credited by the supplied Roadscape Utility Tools.

Standalone live 3D Bench plugin and artwork adaptation: 2026-10-28.

The port retains extracted prop geometry and base-color imagery from the supplied library. Native low-poly road geometry, viewport drawing, live path editing, persistence and generation are implemented in JavaScript. This modified version is not the original Blender add-on and does not reproduce its full Geometry Nodes or shader functionality.

License: GNU GPL version 3 or later, consistent with 3D Bench. The unmodified license supplied with Roadscape is reproduced in COPYING.txt. No warranty is provided.

Rebuild instructions and precise scope are documented in plugins/roadscape/README.md. The generated library records the original blend SHA256, source version and authors.
